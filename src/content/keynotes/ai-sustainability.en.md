Artificial intelligence promises efficiency gains – but consumes considerable amounts of energy itself. For organisations with sustainability goals, this creates a tension that is rarely discussed openly.

This keynote looks at both sides: the energy demand of AI, and how people respond when it is made visible. It draws on my current research on disclosing the energy consumption of AI, published at ACM CHI 2026.
