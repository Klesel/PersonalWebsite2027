Generative AI has gone from research topic to everyday tool in no time. At the same time, there is a lot of uncertainty: what can systems like ChatGPT actually do – and where should you not rely on them?

In this keynote I explain, without jargon, how modern AI systems work and use current research to show what matters when you deploy them. Your audience gets a realistic picture beyond hype and doom – and a basis for making well-founded decisions of their own.
