AI tools are quickly rolled out – but that does not mean they are used well. Between buying a licence and creating real value lie questions of trust, skills, work organisation and leadership.

In this keynote I draw on empirical research to show what distinguishes successful AI adoption, what role explainability plays for trust, and why individual differences decide whether AI really helps in everyday work.
