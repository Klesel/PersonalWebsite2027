I am a Professor of Information Systems in the Faculty of Business and Law at Frankfurt University of Applied Sciences. I am interested in how technology changes the way we work and make decisions – and in what organisations can do to make sure new technologies actually create value.

My current research focuses on artificial intelligence: I study which factors make AI adoption in organisations successful, how explainability affects trust in AI systems, why people use AI with differing effectiveness, and what happens when the energy consumption of AI is made transparent.

In doing so, I combine behavioural research with design-oriented approaches, with a methodological focus on experiments and structural equation modelling. My work has been published in journals including *Internet Research*, *Electronic Markets*, *Business & Information Systems Engineering*, *Communications of the Association for Information Systems* and *The DATA BASE for Advances in Information Systems*, and at conferences such as ACM CHI, ICIS and HICSS.

I received my doctorate from the University of Siegen with a thesis on individual information systems.

Through keynotes and consulting, I pass this knowledge on to companies, public institutions and event organisers – in an accessible, independent way, with the aim that your audience leaves with concrete ideas.
