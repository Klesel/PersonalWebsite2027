## Storing your colour scheme preference

If you switch between the light and dark colour scheme using the button in the header, your choice is saved in your browser's local storage (localStorage) so it is kept for your next visit. This information never leaves your device and is not sent to the server. You can delete it at any time in your browser settings.

The data is deleted once it is no longer needed for handling your enquiry and no statutory retention obligations apply.
